# Flat triple piston extender

Arm along +x at y=0,z=0 (pistons x=0,1,2; tip stone at x=3). Lever at the far north (x=2, z=-24).
Lever ON: tip goes x=3 -> 6. Lever OFF: it returns (3 short pulses pull everything home).

**Status: simulated only (Java 1.20 rules). Not tested in the real game.** Please try it and report back.

Footprint about 24 wide x 38 long. Keep x=4..6 on the arm line free. No slime needed.
Schematic: `flat-arm3.schem` (origin at minimum corner).
