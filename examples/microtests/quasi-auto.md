# One-lever quasi-connectivity test

Paste `quasi-auto.schem` (55x3x5). Start `/doortrace start` over the whole thing, flip the ONE lever at the west end ON, wait 5 s, flip it OFF, wait 5 s, `/doortrace stop`.

Five pistons facing east, west to east: 1 control (dust against it), 2 dust diagonal to the air above it, 3 same + kick (a piston pushes a stone against it), 4 stone above it + dust beside, 5 same + kick.
Simulator: all five extend on ON and retract on OFF. Real game may differ (that is the test).
