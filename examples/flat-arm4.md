# Flat quad piston extender

Arm along +x (y=0, z=0): sticky pistons x=0..3, tip stone at x=4. Lever ON: tip goes x=4 -> 8. OFF: returns via 6 pulses.
**Simulated only (Java 1.20 rules); not tested in the real game.** Keep x=5..8 on the arm line free.
Footprint about 46 x 40 (long delay lines). Lever at the far north end. Schematic: `flat-arm4.schem`.
Generator: `examples/flat-armN.ts` (any N, give it the hold/pulse waveforms).
